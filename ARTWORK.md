# Artwork and reference data

The [MIT license](LICENSE) applies to the Goodhand software and documentation.
It does not grant rights to the artwork, source imagery, private review
archives, or reference datasets in `reference/`. Availability alongside the
software is not permission to redistribute those materials.

A public executable requires separate permission covering the exact final
540-phase artwork it embeds, including extraction from the executable.
Each distribution that includes artwork must carry that artwork's applicable
notice. Source footage, guides, and the reviewed W4 seed are not distribution
inputs and are never needed to build from the final artwork.
